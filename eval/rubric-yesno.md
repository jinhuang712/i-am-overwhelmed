# Rubric, as yes or no questions

A draft. It will replace [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) when every item is converted and the tool can score it. The reason for the change is in [`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md), under "About judging".

Each question names one thing a judge can see. The answer is F when the reply does it, P when it does not, and NA in the case its row names. Judge every final reply of a sample together: a question fails when any turn fails it.

## C. Skimmability

### C1. Point first

| # | F when | NA when |
|---|---|---|
| C1a | The first sentence is a greeting or an acknowledgement | never |
| C1b | The first sentence praises the user or the question | never |
| C1c | The reply gives background before its point | never |
| C1d | The reply lists the steps it took before the result | never |

An opening that says the question again, or says what the reply will do, is allowed. It can help the reader.
