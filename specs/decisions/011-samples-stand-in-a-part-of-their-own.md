# 011. Samples stand in a part of their own

Decided: 2026-11-18

---

## The problem

Rules of style are the least followed. The rule against parallel clauses was broken in 14 of 20 replies, with the plugin and without. A fault that is pointed out is put right at once, since the pointing is near and comes with the case. A rule in the file had no case, because a rule carried no example.

---

## The choice

Three samples of faults of style stand in one part at the end of Wordings, in a code block. The part says that they are samples and not a complete list. Each sample is general: its content comes from food, letters and a door, and from no code and no project.

---

## Not chosen

3 other ways:

- **A sample on the line of its rule**: the owner holds that no example hangs on a rule.
- **A sample under each rule**: the owner found that a file with a sample under every rule reads as a mess.
- **No sample at all**: the owner's fear is a rule so narrow that the model obeys it and fails elsewhere, and a sample named as a sample answers that fear better than a longer rule.

---

## Consequences

A sample stands some lines away from its rule, and whether it binds from there is not measured. A sample may be taken for the only case, which the sentence above the block is there to prevent.
