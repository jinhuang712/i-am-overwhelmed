# 007. Yes or no lines judge a reply, and the 0 to 3 scale is frozen

Decided: 2026-11-13

---

## The problem

A judge gave the same score three times on 54% to 58% of the lines of the 0 to 3 scale. The scale also judged what the agent did, and the owner set the scope to the reply on 2026-11-11.

---

## The choice

The rubric is a list of yes or no questions about a reply, one thing a line. A cheap judge answers a deck three times and the majority counts. It is the default of the eval. The scale is frozen and reads or repeats an eval from before that day.

A line asks what the reader gets and not whether a format of ours is there. A red flag names a harm.

---

## Not chosen

2 other ways:

- **Keep the scale**: a judge gave the same yes or no on 86% to 89% of the lines, against 54% to 58% on the scale.
- **Lines that reward the plugin's own formats**: they gave the plugin points that a reply with no plugin could never earn.

---

## Consequences

A rubric line that changes is checked again before a benchmark, which costs money. A line is parked and never deleted for how often it fires. Replies with no plugin are the second benchmark, since the owner stopped comparing with 0.1.14 on 2026-11-11.
