# 006. A drawn rule has its own heading and no tag

Decided: 2026-11-13

---

## The problem

Each drawing hung from a one-line `[YES]`, such as "End a reply by this tree:". A tree holds as much as three to five rules, and on the page it weighed as much as one bullet between two others.

---

## The choice

A drawn rule has its own heading at the end of its section and no tag. One sentence before it says why it is there and then what to do with it. Every section opens with one sentence in the reader's voice that says why its rules are there.

---

## Not chosen

1 other way:

- **A tagged line above each drawing**: it hid the weight of the drawing.

---

## Consequences

The change is for the reader of the file. No reply was run to measure it.
