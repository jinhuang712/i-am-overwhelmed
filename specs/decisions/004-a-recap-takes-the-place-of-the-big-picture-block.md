# 004. A recap takes the place of the big picture block

Decided: 2026-11-18

---

## The problem

When the big picture changed, a reply opened with a 📍 block: the goal, what was decided and what was open. The block grew with the talk, much of it was not what the owner was on, and at times the owner did not read it. On a clean baseline it also led the agent to list its own advice as decided.

---

## The choice

Every reply after the first opens with `<recap>`: the current state of the work and what requires my attention. The line sets no length. On long work the recap takes a structured form, since the owner found one sentence too little there. The 📍 block, its section and its three emoji left the rule file.

The same day the recap took in four lines that looked back: "say what changed since the last reply", "say again what I need from an earlier reply", the background and the progress line.

---

## Not chosen

4 other ways:

- **Keep the 📍 block**: it is a ledger, and the books belong in a file.
- **A cap of three lines**: the owner asked for no length, since the model is able to judge it.
- **A list of what the recap holds**: each named thing invites a line that fills it.
- **A condition the model judges, such as "when the big picture changes"**: on Sonnet the block was missing in 10 of 10 replies of one task.

---

## Consequences

No reply was run under the line. "Say what changed" had an eval behind it, and whether the recap carries it is not measured. The rubric still has a line that asks for the goal, what the user decided and what is open. The rule "Call a thing decided only when I verbally agreed on it" stays and moved under Replies.
