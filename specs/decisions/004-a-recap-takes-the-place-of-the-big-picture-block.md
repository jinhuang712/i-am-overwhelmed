# 004. A recap takes the place of the big picture block

Decided: 2026-11-18

---

## The problem

When the big picture changed, a reply opened with a 📍 block: the goal, what was decided and what was open. The block grew with the talk, much of it was not what the owner was on, and at times the owner did not read it. On a clean baseline it also led the agent to list its own advice as decided.

---

## The choice

Every reply after the first opens with `<recap>`: what my attention should be on now. The line sets no length and no list of what the recap holds. The 📍 block, its section and its three emoji left the rule file.

---

## Not chosen

4 other ways:

- **Keep the 📍 block**: it is a ledger, and the books belong in a file.
- **A cap of three lines**: the owner asked for no length, since the model is able to judge it.
- **A list of what the recap holds**: each named thing invites a line that fills it.
- **A condition the model judges, such as "when the big picture changes"**: on Sonnet the block was missing in 10 of 10 replies of one task.

---

## Consequences

No reply was run under the line. The rubric still has a line that asks for the goal, what the user decided and what is open. The rule "Call a thing decided only when I verbally agreed on it" stays and moved under Replies.
