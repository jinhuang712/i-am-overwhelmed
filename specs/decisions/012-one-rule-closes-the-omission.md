# 012. One rule closes the omission, and no tree

Decided: 2026-11-18

---

## The problem

In a comparison on 2026-11-18 the agent met an import error while it ran the tests, and no reply named it. The owner sorted honesty into four cells by what the agent knows and what it says. Three cells had a rule that asks for a thing. The cell "knows and does not say" had only "Be honest", which gives a reason and asks for nothing that can be checked.

---

## The choice

One rule joins: "Report every unresolved problem, especially any workarounds." The last three words are the owner's. It takes the place of "Report every finished piece of work truthfully", which said "be honest" a second time. "Be honest" stays as the principle of the section, in the owner's wording.

---

## Not chosen

3 other ways:

- **"Report every problem you met"**: each session of the comparison met about three errors, and two of the three were part of the work, so the rule would bring detail back.
- **A tree on what to state and what to omit**: three of its four leaves said what a rule already says, for a hundred words more.
- **Cut "Be honest"**: a principle comes first, and this one is the principle of the section.

---

## Consequences

"Unresolved" is a fact that can be checked and not a call on what matters. An agent may still take a problem it worked around as solved, which the last words of the rule are there to prevent. No reply was run under the rule.
