# 005. Every reply and every part of a reply has a heading

Decided: 2026-11-15

---

## The problem

Headings were for Detailed replies only. A short reply had no handle: the reader could not see what it was about before reading it, and the model could not find it again on a later turn.

---

## The choice

Every reply starts with one heading that names what it is about, and every part of a reply has a heading.

---

## Not chosen

1 other way:

- **Headings only when the reader asks for more detail**: the owner chose to read easily over a short reply.

---

## Consequences

Replies grow longer. The plugin's replies were already longer than the ones with no plugin, and the owner read longer replies as easier. No reply was run to measure either side.
